The Belief

Your product isn't just code.

It's the thing your customers use to trust you. I care about what happens after it's built — the speed, the feel, the tiny details that make someone say "wow, this actually works."

I've built e-commerce platforms that sell, mobile apps people open daily, and dashboards that make complex data feel simple. Every project, I treat your business like it's mine.

The Belief

Four rules I live by

i.

Speed is a feature, not a bonus

Slow apps and websites lose customers. I optimize from the first line of code — not as an afterthought.

ii.

Design that earns trust

People judge your credibility in 50 milliseconds. I build interfaces that make that first impression count.

iii.

I ship. Then I iterate.

Perfect is the enemy of live. I get you to market fast, then refine based on what actually matters.

iv.

Communication, always

You'll always know where things stand. No ghosting, no surprises — just clear, honest updates.

VII

Questions Readers Ask

Do you build apps for both iOS and Android?

Yes. I build cross-platform apps with React Native and Expo, so iOS and Android ship from a single codebase without sacrificing quality.

Can you build the backend too, or just the frontend?

Both. I build Node.js/Fastify backends, REST APIs and databases with PostgreSQL, Supabase or MongoDB, plus integrations like Paystack and Cloudflare R2.

Will my website be found on Google?

SEO is built in from the start: Next.js metadata, JSON-LD structured data, sitemaps, Core Web Vitals optimization and Google Search Console setup.

How does a project start?

With a discovery call about your goals, users, timeline and budget. Then I scope milestones, build in stages with regular check-ins, launch, and stick around for support.
